Why More Malaysian Durian Farmers Are Turning to Drone Spraying为什么越来越多马来西亚榴莲园主选择无人机喷洒

June 20262026 年 6 月

Durian orchards are some of the hardest terrain in Malaysian agriculture to spray by hand. Many orchards sit on hillsides with uneven, sloped ground, and mature durian trees can tower well above head height — a combination that turns a routine spraying round into a genuinely risky job.榴莲园是马来西亚农业中人工喷洒难度最高的地形之一。许多果园座落于地势起伏不平的山坡上，成熟的榴莲树高度往往远超人的身高——种种因素使得例行喷洒工作变成了真正具有风险的作业。

The problem with manual spraying on slopes山坡人工喷洒的困境

Traditionally, spraying a hillside durian orchard means workers climbing ladders or scaling improvised platforms with a heavy backpack sprayer, often on wet or loose ground. It's slow work — a large orchard can take a crew several days — and coverage is inevitably uneven, since it's simply not practical to reach every branch at height from the ground.传统做法是工人背着沉重的背式喷雾器，爬上梯子或临时搭建的平台进行喷洒，而且地面经常湿滑松软。这不仅耗时——大型果园往往需要一组人力花上好几天才能完成——喷洒覆盖也难免不均匀，因为从地面很难触及每一根高处的树枝。

An ageing workforce makes it harder人力老化让情况更加困难

Many durian orchard owners describe the same challenge: it's getting harder every year to find workers willing to do this kind of physically demanding, higher-risk work. As the existing workforce ages, some seasons end up under-sprayed simply because there weren't enough hands available at the right time.许多榴莲园主都提到同一个困境：愿意从事这种体力消耗大、风险又高的工作的工人一年比一年难找。随着现有人力逐渐老化，有些季节最终会因为缺乏足够人手而喷洒不足。

How drone spraying changes the equation无人机喷洒如何改变现状

A spraying drone flies a pre-planned route above the canopy, adjusting altitude automatically as the terrain rises and falls, and releasing a controlled, even mist across the whole tree — including the upper branches that are hardest to reach by hand. What used to take a full crew several days can often be completed in a single flight session, with nobody standing on a ladder on a slope.喷洒无人机依照预先规划的航线，在树冠上方飞行，并依地形起伏自动调整飞行高度，将受控且均匀的药雾洒遍整棵树——包括人工最难触及的上层枝叶。过去需要整组人力花上好几天才能完成的工作，现在往往一次飞行作业即可完成，也不必再有人爬上山坡的梯子。

What this means for orchard owners这对果园主意味着什么

For many of the orchard owners Drone X works with, the appeal isn't just speed — it's being able to keep spraying on schedule every season, regardless of how many workers are available that week, and knowing nobody is climbing a hillside with a tank on their back to get it done.对许多与 Drone X 合作的果园主而言，吸引人的不只是速度——更重要的是无论当周人手多寡，都能按时完成每一季的喷洒工作，也不必再担心有人背着药桶爬上山坡作业。
